# Thank You 🌙

Thank you for taking the time to explore **Noctara-dots**.

What started as a personal desktop setup slowly became something much bigger. Hours of tweaking configs, testing different ideas, breaking things, fixing them, and chasing that perfect balance between functionality and aesthetics eventually turned into this project.

Creating Noctara has been an incredibly fun journey. From experimenting with window managers and animations to designing a workflow that feels personal, every small improvement made the process more enjoyable.

Linux customization is a special kind of experience. There is always something new to learn, something new to optimize, and somehow there is always one more thing that "just needs a tiny change" before being finished. (Spoiler: it is never actually finished.) ✨

Seeing other people use Noctara, modify it, take inspiration from it, or simply enjoy exploring it makes all the time spent configuring everything worth it.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Whether you are using Noctara as your daily setup or just browsing through the configs for inspiration, I appreciate your support.
